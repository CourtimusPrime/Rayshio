# OpenRouter

These are step-by-step instructions for how to get invoices from OpenRouter.

## How to Find All Invoices

1. Open Chome
2. Visit `https://openrouter.ai/settings/credits`
3. Next to "Recent Transactions" click on the "History" hyperlink
4. You will be redirected to `https://billing.stripe.com/p/session?secret=live...`

## How to Download Invoices

1. Go to `https://billing.stripe.com/p/session?secret=live...` (see instructions above)
2. Click anywhere on the invoice row you want to select
3. You will be redirected to `https://invoice.stripe.com/i/acct_...`
4. Click on "Download invoice"
5. The invoice will land in `~/Downloads/Invoice-*.pdf`

- Type: Software as a Service

## Executive Summary

Rayshio (pronounced like "ratio") is an online software that aggregates invoices from multiple billing accounts into one dashboard. Rashio makes it easy for business leaders to understand their costs and subscribed services, and send invoices directly to their accounting provider. With an MCP connection, Rayshio allows your payment history readable to AI agents.
